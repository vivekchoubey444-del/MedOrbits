## Accuracy and safety

- Requirements vary by destination, institution, programme, applicant circumstances, and intake.
- Immigration, work, insurance, recognition, and entry rules can change; verify them using current official government or institutional sources.
- No guide guarantees admission, funding, visa approval, employment, or a particular immigration outcome.
- Never submit false documents or rely on unverified promises from agents.
- These guides are general information, not legal, immigration, or financial advice.

## Publishing

Check every PDF for readability, printing, metadata, and working links before publishing. Keep completed forms and personal identity, financial, or immigration documents out of this public directory.
