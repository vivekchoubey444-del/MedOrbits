1. Overview

The Applications Checklists directory contains structured, printable resources that help students, graduates, job seekers, and other applicants prepare, review, submit, and track applications.

These resources are designed to reduce avoidable errors, organize supporting documents, monitor deadlines, and support consistent application management across education and career-related processes.

3. Intended Audience

These resources may be used by:

* Students applying to educational institutions and academic programmes.
* Applicants preparing admission, scholarship, or education-related applications.
* Job seekers managing employment and internship applications.
* Graduates organizing professional or further-study applications.
* Parents, guardians, and authorized education advisers assisting applicants.
* Users accessing application guidance through MedOrbits services.

4. Recommended Workflow

1. Prepare: Identify the application purpose, eligibility requirements, deadline, and submission method.
2. Organize: Gather the required information and supporting documents.
3. Review: Check all entries, attachments, dates, and applicable declarations.
4. Submit: Follow the recipient’s official submission instructions and retain confirmation.
5. Follow up: Record acknowledgements, requests for additional information, and decisions.
6. Track: Update the application tracking worksheet until the process is complete.

Applicants must follow the specific requirements of the institution, employer, scholarship provider, or other receiving organization.

5. Document and Publishing Standards

Every published PDF should:

* Use clear headings, concise instructions, and practical checklist items.
* Include printable checkboxes and adequate space for notes where appropriate.
* Distinguish mandatory requirements from optional or conditional items.
* Identify relevant deadlines or academic years when applicable.
* Use consistent filenames and accessible PDF formatting.
* Be tested for readability, printing, and successful downloading.
* Avoid unsupported claims about eligibility, acceptance, or application outcomes.

6. Brand and Accessibility

Apply the MedOrbits Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual identity consistently.

* Maintain strong text contrast and readable typography.
* Use a logical heading hierarchy and uncluttered page layouts.
* Ensure checkboxes and instructions remain legible when printed.
* Do not communicate status through colour alone.
* Keep document layouts suitable for common paper sizes and digital viewing.

7. Privacy and Data Protection

These resources are intended for public distribution. Do not include completed applicant forms, personal identification records, private contact information, passwords, payment credentials, or confidential application data in this directory.

Applicants should store completed worksheets and personal documents securely and share them only with appropriate recipients.

8. Accuracy and Maintenance

Before publishing or updating a resource:

* Verify references to application requirements against relevant official sources.
* Review the document for outdated instructions and incorrect links.
* Confirm that the PDF filename matches the resource linked on the website.
* Test the download and inspect the rendered PDF.
* Replace outdated resources when procedures or requirements change.

9. Disclaimer

These checklists and worksheets are general organizational aids. They are not official application forms, legal advice, or guarantees of eligibility, selection, admission, employment, or approval.

Application requirements, deadlines, supporting documents, and review procedures vary by recipient and may change. Applicants must verify the latest instructions with the relevant official organization before submitting an application.
