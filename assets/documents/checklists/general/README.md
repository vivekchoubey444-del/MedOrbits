# MedOrbits General Checklists

**Directory:** `assets/documents/checklists/general/`

## Purpose

Public, printable checklists for general planning. Resources use the MedOrbits Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual direction.

## Resources

- `general-checklist.pdf` — A reusable checklist for planning, preparation, and completion.
- `personal-document-checklist.pdf` — Inventory important personal records and track secure storage.
- `deadline-tracking-checklist.pdf` — Record important dates and prepare actions ahead of time.
- `document-review-checklist.pdf` — Check documents for accuracy, readability, and safe sharing.
- `payment-safety-checklist.pdf` — Reduce avoidable risks when making online or institutional payments.
- `task-completion-checklist.pdf` — Confirm that a task is complete and its records are in order.

## Publishing and accuracy

- These documents are general planning aids, not official notices or guarantees.
- Verify current requirements, deadlines, fees, eligibility, and procedures with relevant official sources.
- Check PDF readability, printing, file paths, and links before publication.
- Do not place completed forms or personal applicant data in this public directory.
- Review resources when relevant official requirements change.

## Brand and accessibility

- Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual identity.
- Clear headings, printable checkboxes, and high-contrast text.
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.
