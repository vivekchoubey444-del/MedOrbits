# MedOrbits Education Checklists

**Directory:** `assets/documents/checklists/education/`

## Purpose

Public, printable checklists for education planning. Resources use the MedOrbits Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual direction.

## Resources

- `course-selection-checklist.pdf` — Compare courses against academic goals and practical constraints.
- `college-comparison-checklist.pdf` — Compare colleges using consistent, verifiable criteria.
- `university-verification-checklist.pdf` — Verify an institution and programme before committing money or documents.
- `education-document-checklist.pdf` — Organize academic records for education-related processes.
- `study-planning-checklist.pdf` — Create a manageable plan for study, revision, and progress review.
- `education-budget-checklist.pdf` — Estimate the total cost of study and plan funding sources.
- `enrolment-readiness-checklist.pdf` — Prepare for registration and the start of a course.

## Publishing and accuracy

- These documents are general planning aids, not official notices or guarantees.
- Verify current requirements, deadlines, fees, eligibility, and procedures with relevant official sources.
- Check PDF readability, printing, file paths, and links before publication.
- Do not place completed forms or personal applicant data in this public directory.
- Review resources when relevant official requirements change.

## Brand and accessibility

- Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual identity.
- Clear headings, printable checkboxes, and high-contrast text.
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.
