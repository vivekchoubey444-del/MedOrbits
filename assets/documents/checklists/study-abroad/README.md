# MedOrbits Study Abroad Checklists

**Directory:** `assets/documents/checklists/study-abroad/`

## Purpose

Public, printable checklists for study abroad planning. Resources use the MedOrbits Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual direction.

## Resources

- `study-abroad-checklist.pdf` — Coordinate research and preparation for international education.
- `destination-research-checklist.pdf` — Compare countries and cities for study, cost, and practical suitability.
- `university-verification-checklist.pdf` — Verify international institutions, campuses, and programmes before applying.
- `study-abroad-application-checklist.pdf` — Organize institution applications for international study.
- `visa-document-checklist.pdf` — Help organise documents that may be needed for a student visa or permit.
- `study-abroad-budget-checklist.pdf` — Estimate the full cost of international study and prepare a funding plan.
- `accommodation-preparation-checklist.pdf` — Prepare safe, practical accommodation before travelling.
- `pre-departure-checklist.pdf` — Coordinate final tasks before travelling to study abroad.

## Publishing and accuracy

- These documents are general planning aids, not official notices or guarantees.
- Verify current requirements, deadlines, fees, eligibility, and procedures with relevant official sources.
- Check PDF readability, printing, file paths, and links before publication.
- Do not place completed forms or personal applicant data in this public directory.
- Review resources when relevant official requirements change.

## Brand and accessibility

- Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual identity.
- Clear headings, printable checkboxes, and high-contrast text.
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.
