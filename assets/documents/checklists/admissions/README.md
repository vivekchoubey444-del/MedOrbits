Purpose

Public, printable checklists for students and applicants preparing for educational admissions. These resources support admission planning, document preparation, eligibility verification, application submission, counselling, fee payments, and enrolment.

Intended Audience

* Students applying to undergraduate and postgraduate courses.
* Applicants preparing medical, dental, AYUSH, nursing, pharmacy, engineering, management, law, and other education applications.
* Parents and guardians supporting admission preparation.
* Students using MedOrbits education guidance and consultancy services.

Content Standards

Each checklist should:

* Use clear, actionable items and printable checkboxes.
* Distinguish required documents from conditional or institution-specific requirements.
* Provide space for notes, deadlines, status, and completion where appropriate.
* Avoid presenting general guidance as an official admission notification.
* Include the applicable academic year when requirements are time-sensitive.
* Refer applicants to the relevant official institution, counselling authority, or regulator.

Design and Accessibility

* Apply the MedOrbits Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual identity.
* Maintain readable typography, strong contrast, and consistent headings.
* Use accessible PDF formatting and print-friendly layouts.
* Do not rely on colour alone to communicate status or meaning.
* Keep filenames lowercase and hyphen-separated.

Accuracy and Maintenance

Review the resources when admission procedures, eligibility rules, document requirements, counselling processes, or fee-payment instructions change. Check each PDF for readability, accuracy, correct file paths, and working download links before publication.

Privacy and Security

These are public educational resources. Do not include completed applicant forms, personal identification documents, passwords, payment credentials, or confidential student information.

Disclaimer

These checklists are general planning aids and are not substitutes for official admission notices. Eligibility, deadlines, fees, document requirements, and procedures vary by institution, course, applicant category, and academic year. Applicants must verify current requirements through the relevant official sources before applying or making payments.
