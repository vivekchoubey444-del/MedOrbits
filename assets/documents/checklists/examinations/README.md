# MedOrbits Examinations Checklists

**Directory:** `assets/documents/checklists/examinations/`

## Purpose

Public, printable checklists for examinations planning. Resources use the MedOrbits Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual direction.

## Resources

- `examination-registration-checklist.pdf` — Organize examination registration and confirmation steps.
- `examination-document-checklist.pdf` — Prepare records that may be needed for registration or exam day.
- `exam-preparation-checklist.pdf` — Plan revision, practice, materials, and readiness.
- `exam-day-checklist.pdf` — Prepare for a calm, organised examination day.
- `admit-card-verification-checklist.pdf` — Check examination entry details as soon as the admit card is available.
- `examination-result-checklist.pdf` — Review results and identify the correct next steps.
- `counselling-preparation-checklist.pdf` — Prepare for counselling or placement processes following an examination.

## Publishing and accuracy

- These documents are general planning aids, not official notices or guarantees.
- Verify current requirements, deadlines, fees, eligibility, and procedures with relevant official sources.
- Check PDF readability, printing, file paths, and links before publication.
- Do not place completed forms or personal applicant data in this public directory.
- Review resources when relevant official requirements change.

## Brand and accessibility

- Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual identity.
- Clear headings, printable checkboxes, and high-contrast text.
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.
