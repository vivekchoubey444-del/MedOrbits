1. Overview

The Career Checklists directory provides structured, printable resources to help students, graduates, job seekers, and early-career professionals prepare for employment.

These resources support career readiness, resume preparation, cover-letter writing, job searching, interview preparation, job-offer evaluation, and the transition into a first job.

3. Intended Audience

These resources are suitable for:

* Students preparing for internships and placements.
* Fresh graduates entering the job market.
* Job seekers organizing their applications.
* Professionals exploring new employment opportunities.
* Applicants preparing resumes, cover letters, and interviews.
* Individuals preparing to begin their first job.

4. Recommended Career Preparation Workflow

1. Assess readiness: Identify career goals, qualifications, transferable skills, and development needs.
2. Prepare documents: Review the resume and create a role-specific cover letter when required.
3. Search strategically: Identify relevant opportunities and maintain an application record.
4. Prepare for interviews: Research employers, review role requirements, and confirm interview arrangements.
5. Evaluate offers: Clarify compensation, responsibilities, benefits, and employment terms before making a decision.
6. Prepare to start: Organize required documents and understand onboarding instructions.

This workflow is general guidance. The appropriate steps depend on the role, employer, industry, and applicant’s circumstances.

5. Content and Publishing Standards

Each PDF should:

* Use concise, actionable checklist items.
* Include printable checkboxes and space for notes where appropriate.
* Provide practical guidance without guaranteeing employment outcomes.
* Encourage applicants to verify job details directly with the employer.
* Identify relevant dates or versions when information may become outdated.
* Use consistent filenames and accessible PDF formatting.

6. Job-Search Safety

Career resources should encourage users to:

* Verify employers and recruitment agencies before sharing information.
* Review job descriptions and employment terms carefully.
* Be cautious of requests for upfront recruitment fees or suspicious payments.
* Avoid sharing passwords, banking credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information.
* Use trusted channels when submitting personal documents.
* Confirm offer details through independently verified employer contact information when necessary.

7. Brand and Accessibility

Apply the MedOrbits Royal Burgundy and Champagne Gold visual identity consistently.

* Use readable typography, clear headings, and strong contrast.
* Keep layouts uncluttered and suitable for printing.
* Make checkboxes and instructions easy to distinguish.
* Use accessible PDF structure where supported.
* Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.

8. Privacy and Maintenance

These files are intended for public distribution. Do not publish completed resumes, private applicant records, personal identification documents, confidential employer correspondence, or other user-specific information in this directory.

Review documents periodically for outdated guidance, broken links, readability, and download functionality. Update resources when relevant employment practices or safety guidance changes.

9. Disclaimer

These resources provide general career-planning guidance. They do not guarantee interviews, job offers, hiring decisions, salary levels, or employment outcomes. Hiring requirements, compensation, benefits, and employment conditions vary by employer, industry, location, and role. Applicants should independently verify relevant details before accepting an offer or sharing personal information.
