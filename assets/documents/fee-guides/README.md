## Accuracy and safety

- Fee amounts vary by institution, programme, campus, intake, student category, and applicable rules.
- This directory intentionally avoids asserting unverified current fee amounts.
- Use the latest official fee notice, prospectus, counselling authority, or institution payment portal.
- Verify refund, cancellation, loan, scholarship, and payment conditions in writing.
- Never send money to a personal account based solely on an agent's message.
- These guides are general information and do not constitute financial or legal advice.

## Publishing

Check every PDF for readability, printing, metadata, and working links before publishing. Do not store personal financial records or completed applicant forms in this public directory.
