1. Overview

This directory contains admission guides published by MedOrbits to help students and families understand education pathways and prepare for admissions.

Guides may cover:

* MBBS and medical admissions
* BDS and dental admissions
* AYUSH programmes
* Nursing and paramedical courses
* Pharmacy programmes
* Engineering and technology
* Management and business education
* Law and other professional courses
* Postgraduate education
* General admission and counselling preparation

All guides must distinguish general educational guidance from official admission requirements.

3. Guide Content Standards

Where relevant, each admission guide should include:

1. Introduction — course overview and intended audience.
2. Eligibility — academic qualifications and other applicable requirements.
3. Entrance examinations — relevant examinations and official information sources.
4. Admission process — application, counselling, selection, and enrolment steps.
5. Important dates — verified application and counselling deadlines.
6. Required documents — checklist of documents and applicable formats.
7. Fees and expenses — verified fees, currency, academic year, and applicable payment period.
8. Official resources — links to relevant institutions, regulators, and counselling authorities.
9. Last verified date — when the information was checked.
10. Disclaimer — clarification that official notifications take precedence.

Include only sections relevant to the particular course and admission pathway.

4. Accuracy and Verification

* Verify eligibility and admission requirements using relevant official sources.
* Check deadlines against current official notifications before publishing.
* Clearly identify the academic year covered by the guide.
* Do not invent institutions, fees, eligibility rules, or application dates.
* Distinguish historical information from current requirements.
* Update or withdraw outdated guides when necessary.
* Include source links and a verification date for time-sensitive information.

Official notifications and applicable regulations take precedence over general guidance published by MedOrbits.

5. File Naming Conventions

Use lowercase, descriptive, hyphen-separated filenames.

Preferred examples:

* mbbs-admission-guide.pdf
* bds-admission-guide.pdf
* medical-counselling-checklist.pdf
* postgraduate-admission-guide.pdf

Avoid spaces, ambiguous abbreviations, duplicate filenames, and unnecessary version numbers.

If multiple editions must be retained, use a consistent academic-year convention, such as mbbs-admission-guide-2027-28.pdf, and clearly label the applicable year inside the document.

6. Document Format and Accessibility

* Prefer PDF for finalized, downloadable admission guides.
* Use readable typography and a clear heading hierarchy.
* Include page numbers and meaningful document titles.
* Use selectable text rather than image-only pages wherever possible.
* Add accessible links and meaningful link text.
* Check that tables, lists, and long URLs remain readable.
* Ensure the document works on mobile devices and when printed.

7. Public Access and Privacy

This directory is intended for public educational resources.

Do not store private student application documents, identity proofs, passports, financial records, personal contact details, or confidential counselling files here.

Such records must be handled through secure backend storage with appropriate authentication and authorization.

8. Website Integration

When admission guides are linked from MedOrbits pages:

* Use the correct deployed asset path.
* Confirm that the target file exists.
* Provide a descriptive link or download label.
* Identify the course and applicable academic year.
* Keep links updated when documents are replaced or withdrawn.
* Do not display a download link for a file that has not been published.

9. Publishing Checklist

Before publishing a guide:

* [ ]	Content has been reviewed for accuracy.
* [ ]	Academic year and applicability are clear.
* [ ]	Eligibility and deadlines have been verified.
* [ ]	Official source links are included.
* [ ]	Fees and currencies are clearly identified where applicable.
* [ ]	The verification date is recorded.
* [ ]	PDF text, links, tables, and page layout have been checked.
* [ ]	No private or confidential information is included.
* [ ]	The website link resolves correctly.
* [ ]	Outdated versions are clearly identified or removed from active listings.

10. Maintenance

Review time-sensitive guides whenever relevant official authorities publish new admission notifications or regulations.

Keep the README synchronized with the actual files in this directory. Update this document when new resource categories, publishing rules, or file conventions are introduced.
