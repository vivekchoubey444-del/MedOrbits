5. Interview Preparation Framework

Resources should guide candidates through the following stages.

Before the Interview

* Research the employer, role, and job description.
* Review relevant qualifications, projects, and experience.
* Prepare truthful examples demonstrating skills and achievements.
* Practice common questions and role-specific scenarios.
* Confirm the interview date, time zone, location, format, and required documents.
* Test internet connectivity and equipment for virtual interviews.

During the Interview

* Communicate clearly, respectfully, and professionally.
* Listen carefully and ask for clarification when necessary.
* Support answers with relevant examples.
* Describe personal contributions accurately.
* Respect confidentiality and follow the interviewer’s instructions.
* Ask relevant questions about the role and expectations.

After the Interview

* Send a concise thank-you message when appropriate.
* Follow the employer’s stated communication timeline.
* Send a polite follow-up if the expected response date has passed.
* Record feedback and identify areas for improvement.
* Keep applications and interview notes private.

6. Question and Answer Standards

Practice materials should:

* Label questions as examples rather than predictions of a specific employer’s interview.
* Cover general, behavioral, situational, technical, and role-specific questions where relevant.
* Encourage structured answers using the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, and Result.
* Encourage honest descriptions of achievements, limitations, and individual contributions.
* Avoid promoting memorized answers that misrepresent a candidate’s experience.
* Explain that interview formats and assessment criteria vary by employer.

7. Mock Interview Worksheet Standards

The worksheet should provide blank or generic fields for:

* Target role and interview date.
* Practice questions.
* Candidate responses and supporting examples.
* Communication and technical knowledge assessments.
* Strengths and improvement areas.
* Feedback and next steps.

Do not include actual candidate evaluations, identifiable interview notes, or confidential employer information in public templates.

9. Accessibility and Compatibility

* Use readable typography and clear headings.
* Maintain sufficient color contrast.
* Provide selectable text in PDFs wherever possible.
* Ensure documents display correctly on mobile devices.
* Use accessible tables, links, and printable worksheet fields.
* Avoid layouts that cut off text when printed.
* Use plain language and explain specialized interview terminology.

10. Privacy and Security

This directory is intended for public educational resources.

* Do not publish completed mock interview worksheets containing personal information.
* Keep candidate assessments, interview notes, and recruitment records in an appropriate private system.
* Do not publish confidential interview questions obtained through restricted channels.
* Remove unnecessary personal metadata before publication.
* Never include passwords, API keys, access tokens, or other secrets.
* Share personal feedback only with the candidate’s consent and appropriate authorization.
