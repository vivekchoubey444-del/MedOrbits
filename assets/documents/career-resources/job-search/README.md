1. Overview

The job-search/ directory contains practical resources to help students, fresh graduates, and experienced professionals plan their job search, identify suitable opportunities, organize applications, evaluate offers, and recognize potential recruitment scams.

These resources support MedOrbits’ career guidance, job assistance, internship support, and recruitment services.

5. Job Search Workflow

Resources should help candidates follow a structured process.

Step 1 — Define Career Goals

* Identify preferred roles and industries.
* Review qualifications, skills, and experience.
* Establish realistic location, salary, and work-arrangement preferences.

Step 2 — Find Suitable Opportunities

* Use reputable job portals and official employer career pages.
* Review job descriptions and eligibility criteria.
* Check application deadlines and required documents.
* Verify that vacancies are genuine and relevant.

Step 3 — Prepare Applications

* Customize resumes and cover letters for each role.
* Provide accurate information about qualifications and achievements.
* Submit applications through appropriate channels.
* Keep copies of submitted documents where appropriate.

Step 4 — Track Progress

* Record the employer, role, application date, and public vacancy URL.
* Track interview dates and application status.
* Record follow-up dates and relevant next steps.
* Remove outdated entries when no longer needed.

Step 5 — Evaluate Offers

* Review the role, compensation, working hours, location, and responsibilities.
* Understand the distinction between gross salary, take-home pay, and variable compensation.
* Read written terms carefully.
* Clarify probation, notice periods, benefits, and other applicable conditions.
* Seek qualified advice when significant contractual or legal questions arise.

6. Job Application Tracker Standards

The workbook is a reusable template for individual users.

Recommended fields include:

* Employer name.
* Job title.
* Vacancy URL.
* Application date.
* Application status.
* Interview date, if applicable.
* Follow-up date.
* Next action.
* Notes limited to necessary application information.

Do not include passwords, one-time passcodes, bank details, identity numbers, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Privacy requirement: Never publish completed application trackers containing personal or confidential information in this public assets directory.

7. Employer and Vacancy Verification

Candidates should independently verify:

* The employer’s identity and official website.
* The vacancy through an official careers page or authorized recruiter.
* The recruiter’s email domain and contact information.
* The job description, eligibility criteria, and application process.
* Written offer details and the identity of the issuing organization.

Requests for payment to secure a job, interview, offer letter, or guaranteed placement are significant warning signs. Verify any unexpected fees independently before proceeding.

HTTPS alone does not establish that an employer or vacancy is legitimate.

8. Content Quality Standards

All resources must:

* Use accurate, clear, and professional language.
* Distinguish general advice from employer-specific requirements.
* Avoid fabricated vacancies, employers, salaries, or recruitment claims.
* Avoid guaranteeing interviews, offers, or employment.
* Identify external resources accurately.
* Include practical verification and privacy guidance.
* Be reviewed when relevant procedures or external links change.

Do not describe a vacancy as current unless its availability has been verified through an appropriate source.

11. Privacy and Security

This directory is intended for public educational resources.

* Keep personal application records outside the public assets directory.
* Never publish completed trackers, private recruiter correspondence, or confidential employment offers.
* Remove unnecessary document metadata before publication.
* Never include passwords, API keys, access tokens, or other secrets.
* Use access-controlled storage for private recruitment and candidate records.
* Share personal information only when necessary and through appropriate channels.

14. Scope and Disclaimer

These resources provide general educational and career guidance. They do not guarantee the availability or legitimacy of a particular vacancy, job interviews, employment offers, or recruitment outcomes.

Candidates should verify opportunities directly with employers or authorized recruitment organizations and seek qualified advice for significant legal or contractual questions.
