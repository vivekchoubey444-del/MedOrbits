1. Overview

The workplace-skills/ directory contains practical learning resources that help students, fresh graduates, interns, and early-career professionals develop the communication, collaboration, and professional habits needed to navigate workplace environments.

These resources support MedOrbits’ career guidance, job assistance, internship support, and recruitment services.

5. Core Skill Areas

Professional Communication

Resources should cover:

* Clear, concise, and respectful communication.
* Professional emails, messages, and meeting participation.
* Active listening and appropriate questioning.
* Constructive feedback and conflict resolution.
* Adapting communication to the audience and context.

Workplace Etiquette

Resources should cover:

* Punctuality and reliability.
* Respectful professional interactions.
* Appropriate meeting and communication practices.
* Responsible use of workplace equipment and digital tools.
* Confidentiality and compliance with organizational policies.
* Professional conduct in remote, hybrid, and in-person settings.

Teamwork and Collaboration

Resources should cover:

* Defining responsibilities and shared goals.
* Coordinating tasks and communicating progress.
* Managing disagreements constructively.
* Giving and receiving feedback respectfully.
* Taking accountability for individual contributions.
* Escalating blockers and seeking assistance when appropriate.

First-Job Readiness

Resources should help candidates prepare for:

* Reviewing the employment offer and joining instructions.
* Organizing required documents securely.
* Understanding role expectations and reporting relationships.
* Preparing questions for managers or mentors.
* Learning relevant workplace policies.
* Setting initial learning goals and requesting feedback.

6. Content Quality Standards

All resources must:

* Use clear, inclusive, and professional language.
* Provide practical examples relevant to real workplace situations.
* Present recommendations as general guidance rather than universal rules.
* Recognize that expectations vary by industry, employer, culture, and working arrangement.
* Encourage respectful communication and professional accountability.
* Avoid stereotypes and discriminatory assumptions.
* Never disclose confidential workplace examples or identifiable employee information.
* Avoid guaranteeing promotion, employment, or professional success.

7. File Naming and Version Control

Use lowercase, descriptive, hyphen-separated filenames.

Examples:

* professional-communication-guide.pdf
* workplace-etiquette-guide.pdf
* teamwork-and-collaboration-guide.pdf
* first-job-readiness-checklist.pdf

Review files before replacing approved versions. Record review dates and version information where appropriate.

8. Accessibility and Compatibility

* Use readable typography and meaningful headings.
* Maintain sufficient color contrast.
* Provide selectable text in PDFs wherever possible.
* Ensure documents display correctly on mobile devices.
* Use descriptive hyperlinks and accessible tables.
* Make checklists easy to print and use digitally.
* Explain specialized terminology in plain language.

9. Privacy and Security

This directory is intended for public educational materials.

* Do not publish internal company policies without authorization.
* Do not include confidential emails, workplace disputes, or private employee records.
* Use fictional or anonymized examples.
* Remove unnecessary personal metadata before publication.
* Never include passwords, API keys, access tokens, or other secrets.
* Store private training records and employee information in an appropriate access-controlled system.

10. Website Integration

Resources may be linked from MedOrbits career guidance, job assistance, internship, and professional development pages.

Integration requirements:

* Provide descriptive titles and short summaries.
* Display file formats and file sizes where practical.
* Verify download paths before publication.
* Ensure mobile and desktop compatibility.
* Apply access controls to non-public resources.
* Update links whenever documents are moved or renamed.

12. Scope and Disclaimer

These resources provide general educational and professional development guidance. They do not replace an employer’s official policies, mandatory training, professional regulations, or applicable workplace requirements.

Users should follow relevant organizational procedures and seek guidance from an appropriate supervisor or qualified professional when necessary.
