1. Overview

The internships/ directory contains educational guides, checklists, and career resources to help students, fresh graduates, and early-career professionals explore internship opportunities and prepare for the application process.

These resources support MedOrbits’ career guidance, internship assistance, job-readiness, and recruitment services.

5. Application Guidance

Resources should explain how to:

1. Identify internships relevant to academic qualifications and career goals.
2. Review eligibility criteria, responsibilities, duration, location, and application deadlines.
3. Prepare a role-specific resume and cover letter.
4. Submit applications through verified employer channels.
5. Prepare for interviews and practical assessments.
6. Follow up professionally after applying.
7. Review an offer before accepting it.
8. Maintain records of applications, interviews, and outcomes.

6. Internship Evaluation Standards

Encourage candidates to verify the following before accepting an internship:

* Employer identity and official contact details.
* Internship responsibilities and expected learning outcomes.
* Supervisor, mentor, or reporting structure.
* Start date, duration, working hours, and location.
* Compensation, stipend, expenses, or unpaid status.
* Relevant written terms and conditions.
* Any academic credit or institutional approval requirements.
* Document requests, fees, and other commitments.

Candidates should exercise caution when asked to pay recruitment fees, provide unnecessary sensitive information, or accept unclear working arrangements.

Do not make unsupported legal claims. Applicable requirements may vary by jurisdiction and internship arrangement.

7. Content Quality Standards

All published resources must:

* Use accurate, clear, and professional language.
* Provide practical, actionable guidance.
* Avoid fabricated internship openings, employers, qualifications, or experience.
* Avoid guaranteeing internship placement or future employment.
* Distinguish general guidance from employer-specific requirements.
* Use examples that do not expose real candidates’ personal information.
* Be reviewed when relevant application procedures or external links change.

8. File Naming and Version Control

Use lowercase, descriptive, hyphen-separated filenames.

Examples:

* internship-application-guide.pdf
* internship-interview-guide.pdf
* internship-preparation-checklist.pdf
* internship-resume-checklist.pdf

Review documents before replacing approved versions. Record version and review information where appropriate.

9. Accessibility and Compatibility

* Use readable typography and clear headings.
* Maintain sufficient contrast.
* Provide selectable text in PDFs wherever possible.
* Ensure documents display correctly on mobile devices.
* Use descriptive hyperlinks and accessible tables.
* Avoid unnecessary formatting that makes checklists difficult to print or complete.

10. Privacy and Security

This directory is intended for public educational materials.

* Do not store candidate resumes, identity documents, academic records, internship offers, or private correspondence here.
* Never publish confidential employer or candidate information.
* Remove unnecessary personal metadata before publication.
* Never include credentials, API keys, access tokens, or passwords.
* Store private applications and recruitment records in an appropriate access-controlled system.

11. Website Integration

Resources may be linked from MedOrbits pages covering career guidance, internships, job assistance, student services, and professional development.

Integration requirements:

13. Scope and Disclaimer

These resources provide general educational and career guidance. They do not guarantee internship opportunities, selection, compensation, academic credit, or employment.

Candidates should verify requirements directly with the employer and, where applicable, their educational institution or the relevant official authority.
