Make considered decisions

Choice filling needs careful research.

Before submitting preferences, compare the programme, institution, location, total costs, recognition status, available facilities and any applicable service or licensing requirements.

Do not rely on unofficial seat claims or verbal assurances. Review the counselling authority's rules for choice locking, editing, upgrades, withdrawal and later rounds.

Important checks

Small errors can affect an application. Keep a clear record of each official step and deadline.

Deadlines

Check the official schedule regularly. Registration, choice filling, locking and reporting can have separate deadlines.

Correct Information

Official Payments

Pay only through the authorized portal or method and retain transaction receipts. Be cautious of personal-account payment requests.

Quick answers

Counselling FAQs

Is the counselling process the same for every course?

No. Procedures, eligibility, rounds, fees and reporting rules can differ by course, institution, state and counselling authority. Follow the relevant current official notice.

How should I fill my choices?

Research each option carefully and arrange preferences according to your own priorities and the applicable rules. Review whether choices can be edited or locked before the deadline.

Does registration guarantee a seat?

No. Registration alone does not guarantee allotment or admission. Outcomes depend on official eligibility, rank or merit, available seats, preferences and applicable rules.

What should I do after seat allotment?

Read the official allotment instructions immediately and complete the required acceptance, fee payment, document verification and reporting steps by the specified deadline.

Can MedOrbits guarantee admission?

No. MedOrbits provides general information and guidance resources; admission outcomes are determined by the relevant authorities and institutions.
