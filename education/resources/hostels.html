Before you decide

Your accommodation checklist

Visit in person where possible, inspect the room and read all terms before making a payment.

01 · Verify

Confirm the provider

Verify the owner or institution, exact address, contact details and authority to offer the accommodation.

02 · Inspect

Check the premises

Review room condition, ventilation, sanitation, water, fire exits and the condition of shared spaces.

03 · Read

Understand the agreement

Review rent, deposit, included services, house rules, notice period and refund terms in writing.

04 · Keep records

Save payment proof

Use traceable payment methods and retain receipts, the signed agreement and provider communications.

Quick answers

Hostel and accommodation FAQs

Does MedOrbits guarantee a hostel room?

No. Availability, allotment, facilities and prices are determined by the accommodation provider or institution. Confirm availability directly before making plans.

What should I check before paying a deposit?

Verify the provider and property, inspect the accommodation if possible, read the agreement and refund terms, and obtain a written receipt for every payment.

What costs should I compare?

Compare rent, deposit, meals, utilities, internet, laundry, transport, maintenance and any one-time charges over the same time period.

Should I choose a college hostel or private accommodation?

Compare location, availability, costs, rules, facilities, support and contract conditions. The best option depends on your circumstances and the specific provider.

How can I ask about accommodation guidance?

Use the Contact page to share your study destination, institution or city, expected move-in date and accommodation preferences.
