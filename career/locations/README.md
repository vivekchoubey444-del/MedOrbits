## Shared design and assets
Pages reuse the MedOrbits homepage's announcement bar, opening animation, header, navigation, footer and shared scripts. The visual theme is **Rosewood Elite**: imperial aubergine, rosewood and antique gold. Images are referenced under `/assets/images/`; ensure those assets exist.

## Integration notes
These are general location guides, not a live vacancy feed. Job availability and requirements depend on each employer and listing. Confirm the exact worksite, work arrangement and written offer terms before making commitments.
