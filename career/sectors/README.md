# Career Sectors

This directory contains the sector landing page and individual career-sector guides for MedOrbits.

## Files
- `index.html` — overview of career sectors.
- `healthcare.html` — healthcare role families and application checks.
- `it.html` — information technology career guide.
- `finance.html` — banking and finance career guide.
- `hospitality.html` — hospitality and tourism career guide.
- `aviation.html` — aviation and airport operations guide.
- `manufacturing.html` — manufacturing and engineering guide.
- `logistics.html` — logistics and supply-chain guide.
- `others.html` — other sectors and industries.

## Shared design and assets
Pages reuse the MedOrbits homepage's announcement bar, opening animation, header, navigation, footer and shared scripts. The visual theme is **Rosewood Elite**: imperial aubergine, rosewood and antique gold. Images are referenced under `/assets/images/`; ensure those assets exist.

## Integration notes
Job links point to `/career/jobs.html`. These pages are informational guides and do not imply that a vacancy is currently open or guarantee employment. Verify regulated-role requirements with the relevant authority and each vacancy with the employer.
