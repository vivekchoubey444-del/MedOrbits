# Incident Response

## Initial Response

1. Acknowledge the alert and identify an incident lead.
2. Assess severity, affected services, user impact, and data/security risk.
3. Preserve relevant logs and timestamps while protecting sensitive information.
4. Stabilize the service using approved mitigations.
5. Communicate status through the designated channels.
6. Verify recovery and monitor for recurrence.
7. Record a timeline, root cause, impact, and corrective actions.

## Security Incidents

For suspected credential exposure, unauthorized access, or data disclosure, restrict access, preserve evidence, rotate affected credentials, and follow the organization's security and legal notification procedures. Do not delete evidence or make unsupported public claims.

## Review

Run a blameless post-incident review for significant incidents. Assign corrective actions with owners and due dates, and update monitoring, tests, and runbooks.
