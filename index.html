Helpful information

Frequently asked questions.

General information to help you navigate education and career resources.

What can I explore on MedOrbits?

You can explore education pathways, courses, college resources, admissions, scholarships and career services available through the website.

Where can I find MBBS information?

Visit the MBBS in India page to review the resources available there.

How can I check admission eligibility?

Review current official examination bulletins, institutional requirements and applicable counselling notices. Requirements can change between admission cycles.

Does MedOrbits guarantee admission or employment?

No. Admissions, employment, interviews, selection and other outcomes depend on applicable rules, eligibility, institutions, employers and other factors. Outcomes are not guaranteed.

How do I contact MedOrbits?

Use the contact page or WhatsApp links in the footer to enquire about available services.
