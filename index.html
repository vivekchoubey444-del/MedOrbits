How do I find a suitable course?

Start with the course categories, review eligibility requirements and compare relevant institutions and career paths.

Can MedOrbits help with MBBS options in India or abroad?

Explore the relevant MBBS pages and request guidance. Confirm current admission rules, university recognition, fees and licensing requirements with official sources.

Where can I find information about fees and scholarships?

Visit the fees and scholarships resources. Costs, eligibility, deadlines and funding conditions vary, so confirm details with the institution or scholarship provider.

Does MedOrbits provide career and recruitment support?

Explore the career section for job resources, internships, resume support and recruitment services. Specific services and availability may vary.

How can I contact MedOrbits?

Use the Contact page or submit an enquiry through the Apply page to share your education or career goals.
