MedOrbits Support Guide

This document explains how to request help with MedOrbits. Add verified support channels and service commitments before publishing this file.

Getting Help

For general questions, account issues, course or admission guidance, job-service questions, or website problems, use a support contact or help-centre link published in the root README.md or on the official MedOrbits website.

Do not rely on an email address, phone number, response time, or support link unless it has been officially published and verified.

What to Include in a Support Request

To help the support team understand your issue, provide:

• A short, clear subject.
• A description of the problem or question.
• The page, feature, or service involved.
• The steps that led to the issue and what you expected to happen.
• The approximate date and time, including your time zone, if relevant.
• A screenshot or error message, after removing private information.
• Your application or support reference number, if one was issued and it is safe to share.

Protect Your Information

Never send passwords, one-time passcodes, full payment-card details, API keys, session tokens, or other authentication secrets.

Only provide identity documents, academic records, financial information, or other sensitive documents through an official, secure MedOrbits workflow when they are specifically required. Do not attach them to a public issue or discussion.

If you suspect unauthorized access to your account, say so in your support request and change your password through the official site if you can do so safely.

Security Vulnerabilities

Do not report suspected security vulnerabilities through ordinary public support requests, public issues, or social media. Follow the private reporting instructions in SECURITY.md. If no private security contact is published, ask a maintainer for a private reporting channel before sending technical details.

Bug Reports and Feature Requests

For website bugs or feature suggestions, use the project’s documented issue tracker or support channel, if available. Include reproduction steps and sanitized error details. Do not publish confidential information or details that could expose another person’s account or data.

Applications, Payments, and Documents

When contacting support about an application, payment, subscription, or uploaded document:

• Include the relevant reference number where available.
• State the date and describe the issue clearly.
• Do not send full card details or authentication credentials.
• Do not assume that submitting a request changes an application status, cancels a subscription, initiates a refund, or reverses a payment. Wait for confirmation through an official channel.

Applicable service terms and refund policies govern eligibility and processing. This guide does not promise a particular outcome or turnaround time.

Response Expectations

Support availability, operating hours, response times, and escalation procedures should be stated only in official service information. This document does not establish a guaranteed response time or service-level agreement.

If your issue remains unresolved, follow the escalation instructions provided in the original support response or the official MedOrbits website, if available.

Before Publishing

Project maintainers should complete and verify the following:

☐ Add the official support email address or help-centre URL.
☐ Add official phone or messaging channels, if offered.
☐ Publish support operating hours and expected response times, if applicable.
☐ Document escalation procedures for unresolved requests.
☐ Link relevant privacy, terms, refund, and service policies.
☐ Confirm that every listed support channel is active and controlled by MedOrbits.
