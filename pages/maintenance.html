Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about website maintenance.

Why is the MedOrbits website under maintenance?

Maintenance may be used to improve website performance, reliability, security and user experience. This page does not specify individual technical changes.

When will the website be available again?

A confirmed restoration time has not been published here. Please revisit the website later or contact support for assistance.

Can I still contact MedOrbits?

You can try emailing support@medorbits.in or contacting the team through the WhatsApp links on this page. Contact channels may have separate availability.

What if I was completing an application or payment?

Do not repeat a payment until you have checked its status with your payment provider or the relevant official support channel. Keep your transaction reference, but never share OTPs, passwords or complete card details.

Will my information be safe?

This page cannot confirm the operational status of individual systems or transactions. Use official channels, avoid sending sensitive information through chat and review the MedOrbits Privacy Policy for information about personal data handling.

How do I report a page that is not working?

Visit the Contact page if it is available, or email support@medorbits.in. Include the affected page URL, a short description of the issue and the approximate time it occurred. Do not include passwords or OTPs.
