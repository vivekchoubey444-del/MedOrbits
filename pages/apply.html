Quick answers

Guidance request FAQs

What should I include in my enquiry?

Provide a reachable contact method, the service you are interested in and a short explanation of your goal or questions. Only include information needed to handle the enquiry.

Does submitting this form mean I am enrolled or registered?

No. This form is an enquiry only. Any application, registration, service agreement or payment process would need to be communicated and completed separately where applicable.

Can I ask about careers or recruitment instead of education?

Yes. Choose career guidance, jobs and internships, recruitment or employer services from the enquiry-type menu.

What if the form does not submit?

The form requires a working backend endpoint at /api/applications. If the endpoint is not configured, use the Contact page while the technical setup is completed.

Are admissions, jobs or outcomes guaranteed?

No. Outcomes depend on institutions, employers and other relevant authorities. Verify current eligibility, recognition, costs, deadlines and terms before making decisions.
