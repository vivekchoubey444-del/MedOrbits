Quick answers

Career FAQs

Useful information about the career directory.

Are the opportunities shown here live vacancies?

No. The cards show career categories and resource pages. A verified jobs API or backend is required to display current vacancies and application deadlines.

How can I request career guidance?

Use the Get Guidance button to open the MedOrbits application page. The form needs a configured submission service to save and deliver enquiries.

Can students use this career directory?

Yes. Students can explore internship resources, career planning, resume preparation and early-career guidance.

Does MedOrbits guarantee a job or placement?

No. Career resources and recruitment support do not guarantee employment, interviews, selection, salary or placement.

How can employers enquire about recruitment?

Visit the employer services page or submit an enquiry through the application form. Any service is subject to the actual services and terms offered by MedOrbits.
