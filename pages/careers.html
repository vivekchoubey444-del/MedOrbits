Quick answers

Career services FAQs

Review these basics before exploring specific opportunities or services.

Ask a question →
Does MedOrbits guarantee a job or internship?

No. Job selection, interviews, internships, compensation and other outcomes depend on the employer and candidate. No outcome is guaranteed.

Can I get help preparing my resume?

Visit the Resume Support page to review available resources and contact MedOrbits to ask about the support currently offered.

Can students explore internships?

Use the Internships page to review available information. Verify the employer, duties, duration, compensation and any other conditions directly before accepting an opportunity.

Can employers enquire about recruitment services?

Yes. Use the Contact page and select or mention recruitment services, including the role types and hiring needs you want to discuss.

How should I protect myself when applying?

Check employer contact details and the official application channel. Be cautious of requests for upfront payment, passwords, one-time codes or unnecessary sensitive documents.
