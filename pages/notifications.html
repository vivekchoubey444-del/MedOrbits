Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Understand how this notification page works and what it can manage.

Are these live account notifications?

No. The items included in this page are illustrative examples. Real account-specific notifications require a backend API, authenticated user identity and notification data source.

Why does my unread count change?

The count updates when you mark an item as read or unread, change the filter, or clear the list. The demo state can be saved in this browser if local read-status storage is enabled.

Will notifications sync across my devices?

No. Browser-local read status does not synchronise across devices or accounts. Cross-device synchronisation requires a server-side notification service and an authenticated account.

How do browser notifications work?

Supported browsers may ask for permission. Permission alone does not create live alerts: the site must also implement a notification delivery mechanism, such as a service worker and push subscription connected to a server.

Can I manage email alerts here?

Not yet. This page does not update server-side email subscriptions. Contact MedOrbits support to request help with email preferences.

What if a notification is missing or incorrect?

Contact support with the relevant page or issue details. Do not include passwords, OTPs or complete payment-card information in your message.
