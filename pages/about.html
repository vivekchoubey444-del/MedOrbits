Who we are

A clearer way to explore what comes next.

Choosing a course, comparing institutions, understanding admissions or preparing for work can involve many decisions and sources of information. MedOrbits brings these themes together in one place to help students, graduates, job seekers and employers begin their research.

Our platform is designed around discovery and preparation: explore relevant pathways, understand the questions to ask, organize your next steps and refer to official institutions or authorities for current requirements.

Principles that guide us

Useful information. Clear expectations.

Education and employment decisions can have important financial and professional consequences. We believe resources should help people ask informed questions and understand what still needs to be checked.

Clarity over complexity Research before decisions Practical next steps Realistic expectations Respect for individual goals Official-source verification
A note on accuracy

Your decisions deserve verified details.

Course availability, admission criteria, licensing rules, fees, scholarships, vacancies and recruitment outcomes can change. Always confirm the latest information with the relevant official institution, regulator, scholarship provider or employer.

MedOrbits does not guarantee admission, recognition, employment, funding or any particular outcome. Any specific guidance or service is subject to its stated scope and applicable requirements.

What is MedOrbits?

MedOrbits is a connected starting point for exploring education pathways, admission resources, career preparation and recruitment services.

Who can use MedOrbits?

Students, prospective applicants, graduates, job seekers and employers can explore the resources relevant to their goals.

Does MedOrbits guarantee admission or a job?

No. Admission, employment, scholarships, recognition and other outcomes depend on the relevant institution, authority, employer and individual circumstances.

Where should I verify fees, eligibility and recognition?

Confirm current requirements directly with the institution, regulator, official counselling authority or other responsible organization.

How can I get in touch?

Visit the Contact page or submit an enquiry through the Request Guidance page to share your goals and questions.
