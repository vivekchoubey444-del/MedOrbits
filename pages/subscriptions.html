Subscription FAQs

Questions About Our Plans

Important information about prices, payments and subscription access.

Are the prices on this page final?
No. Student Plus and Career Pro currently display illustrative amounts for the page layout. Replace them with approved MedOrbits pricing, billing intervals and applicable taxes before publishing for customers.
Can I pay for a subscription on this page?
Not with this standalone HTML page. Payment requires a server-side checkout integration with your chosen provider, secure order creation, payment verification and subscription status management.
Does clicking a plan activate my membership?
No. The current plan buttons open registration or contact links. A subscription should only become active after the backend verifies the payment or confirms an approved free-plan registration.
Can I cancel a paid subscription?
Cancellation terms should be clearly documented in the subscription agreement and implemented in the account dashboard or support workflow. Publish your actual cancellation and renewal policy before accepting payments.
Are counselling and recruitment included in membership?
Not automatically. Personalised counselling, recruitment and other professional services may have separate eligibility, availability, scope and charges. Confirm each service's terms before purchase.
Will a subscription guarantee admission or a job?
No. A subscription provides only the benefits explicitly included in the relevant plan. Admission, scholarships, employment, recruitment outcomes and visa decisions depend on external institutions, employers and authorities.
How do I ask about institutional or business plans?
Contact MedOrbits with your organisation's requirements, expected users, required features and preferred billing arrangements. Institutional pricing should be quoted and agreed separately.
