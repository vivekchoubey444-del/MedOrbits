Before you contact us

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about contacting MedOrbits.

How can I contact MedOrbits?

You can message either of the WhatsApp numbers listed above, email support@medorbits.in or use the enquiry form on this page.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Include your name, a working email address, the relevant enquiry category and a clear description of your question. Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive information.

Can I ask about admissions and medical courses?

Yes. You can enquire about course discovery, college research, admission resources and counselling information. Verify current eligibility, recognition, fees and admission decisions with the relevant official authority.

Can employers contact MedOrbits about recruitment?

Yes. Select recruitment or employer services in the enquiry form and describe your hiring requirements or partnership proposal.

Does submitting this form automatically send my message?

The form currently prepares an email in your email application. You must review and send that email. Direct server-side submission requires a configured contact API.

Does MedOrbits guarantee admission or employment?

No. Admission, scholarships, employment and other outcomes depend on the relevant institutions, authorities, employers and individual circumstances.
