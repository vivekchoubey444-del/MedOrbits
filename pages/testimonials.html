How feedback is handled

Trust starts with transparency.

Testimonials should represent real experiences and should not imply that every person will receive the same outcome. Admission decisions, scholarships, visas, job offers and other results depend on the relevant institutions, employers and authorities.

  • Obtain consent before publishing names, photos or identifying details.
  • Keep the original meaning of feedback intact when editing for clarity.
  • Disclose material relationships or incentives where applicable.
  • Never promise admission, employment or a particular result based on a testimonial.

Would you like to share your experience?

Send feedback through our contact page. Include the service area and your comments, and tell us whether you consent to any part being considered for publication. Do not include confidential documents or sensitive personal information.

Testimonials FAQs

Are the testimonials currently shown real customer reviews?

No. The cards above are labelled placeholders to show the intended layout. Replace them with verified feedback from real people before publishing the page as a testimonials page.

Can my name or photograph appear on the website?

Identifying details should only be published with your informed permission. You can ask to provide feedback without having your full identity displayed.

Does a testimonial guarantee similar results?

No. Individual experiences are not a promise of admission, scholarships, visas, employment or any particular outcome.

How can I send feedback?

Use the Contact page and describe the service and experience you wish to share. Indicate clearly whether you consent to publication.

Can I request a correction or removal?

Contact MedOrbits with enough information to identify the published feedback and explain the correction or removal request.
