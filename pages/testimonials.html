Frequently Asked Questions

Testimonials & Reviews FAQs

Understand how reviews can be collected, displayed and handled.

Are the testimonials on this page verified customer reviews?
The included cards are sample placeholders, not actual customer reviews. Replace them with genuine feedback and obtain the appropriate permission before presenting them as real testimonials.
Can I submit feedback about a MedOrbits service?
Yes, you can prepare feedback using the form on this page. The current standalone page does not submit or save the review. A backend endpoint must be configured to receive submissions.
Will my review be published immediately?
It should not be published automatically. A responsible review workflow should validate the submission, apply moderation rules, protect personal information and confirm the review is appropriate for publication.
Will my email address appear with my review?
This page asks for an email address as a form field, but it does not save or publish submissions. In a connected implementation, the email should be protected and excluded from public review data.
Can I edit or request removal of a published review?
A production review system should provide a way to contact support about corrections or removal requests, subject to its published privacy and review policies.
Does a positive review guarantee an admission or job?
No. Testimonials describe individual experiences and do not guarantee admissions, scholarships, visas, jobs, recruitment outcomes or any particular result.
