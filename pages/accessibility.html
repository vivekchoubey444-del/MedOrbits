Accessibility Statement

MedOrbits aims to make education, admissions, career information and student-support resources usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies.

⌨

Keyboard access

We aim to make navigation and key actions accessible without a mouse.

◉

Readable content

We aim for clear headings, understandable language and visible focus indicators.

◎

Assistive technology

We aim to support screen readers and other assistive browsing tools.

1. Our commitment

MedOrbits is committed to improving the accessibility and usability of its website. We want visitors to discover information and use our online services regardless of their abilities, devices or preferred browsing methods.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. Some pages, documents, embedded services or older content may not yet provide the same experience for every visitor.

2. Accessibility practices

Our development approach aims to include practices such as:

  • Using meaningful page titles, headings and semantic HTML landmarks.
  • Providing text alternatives for informative images where appropriate.
  • Maintaining readable text and clear visual focus indicators.
  • Supporting keyboard navigation for interactive controls.
  • Labelling form fields and providing understandable instructions.
  • Making layouts responsive for mobile, tablet and desktop screens.
  • Respecting reduced-motion preferences for supported animations.
  • Using descriptive link text and accessible names for controls.

These are development goals, not a claim that every page has passed an independent accessibility audit.

3. Keyboard navigation

Where supported, use the Tab key to move between interactive elements, Shift + Tab to move backwards, and Enter or Space to activate controls. Use arrow keys where a component specifically supports them.

A "Skip to main content" link is provided to help visitors bypass repeated navigation. If a control cannot be reached or operated using your keyboard, please report the page and the steps you took.

4. Screen readers and assistive technologies

We aim to use semantic HTML, meaningful labels and appropriate accessible names to help screen-reader users understand page structure and interactive controls. Compatibility may vary with browser, device, assistive technology and third-party content.

If you encounter an unlabeled button, missing image description, confusing reading order or inaccessible document, please report it.

5. Documents and third-party content

Some resources may be provided as PDF documents, external links, embedded tools or third-party services. We may not control the accessibility of all external content. If a resource is difficult to access, identify the page or document in your feedback so we can investigate an accessible alternative where reasonably possible.

6. Known limitations and continuous improvement

Automated checks alone cannot identify every accessibility barrier. We aim to improve the site over time and may review reports alongside keyboard checks, assistive-technology testing and other quality checks.

We do not claim full conformance with a particular accessibility standard unless and until that conformance has been assessed and verified.

7. Feedback and accessibility assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please use the contact links in the footer of this page. Helpful details include the page URL, the action you were trying to complete, your browser and device, and any assistive technology involved.

Please do not send passwords, OTPs or sensitive personal documents when reporting an accessibility issue.

8. Statement updates

This statement may be updated as the website and its accessibility practices evolve. Please revisit this page for the latest version.
