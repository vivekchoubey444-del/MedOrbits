Code of Conduct

Our Commitment

MedOrbits is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in its project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and community events.

We welcome contributions regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint. Participation is expected to be professional and considerate.

Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that help create a positive environment include:

• Being respectful, patient, and constructive.
• Discussing ideas and technical decisions without personal attacks.
• Giving and receiving feedback professionally.
• Considering different perspectives and experiences.
• Using welcoming and inclusive language.
• Respecting privacy and avoiding the publication of personal or confidential information.
• Focusing criticism on work, behavior, or proposals rather than a person’s identity or character.

Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

• Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or threats.
• Insults, personal attacks, or deliberately humiliating conduct.
• Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention in project spaces.
• Publishing another person’s private information without permission.
• Trolling, sustained disruption, or repeated bad-faith engagement.
• Retaliation against someone who raises a concern or participates in a review.
• Encouraging or engaging in unauthorized access, disclosure of sensitive data, or other harmful activity.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions when someone is representing or participating in the MedOrbits project. It applies to maintainers, contributors, reviewers, and community members.

Reporting

Report conduct that violates this policy to the project maintainers through a private contact method published in the repository’s README.md or on the official MedOrbits website.

Please include a description of the incident, relevant links or dates, and any context that can be shared safely. Do not post private reports publicly or include passwords, access tokens, identity documents, or unrelated personal information.

If no private reporting contact is currently published, ask a maintainer for a private channel before sharing sensitive details. Do not use this process as a substitute for the security vulnerability reporting instructions in SECURITY.md.

Handling Reports

Maintainers should review reports promptly, fairly, and as confidentially as practical. They may request additional information, consult relevant participants, and take action they consider appropriate to protect the community.

Possible responses include a warning, removal of harmful content, restrictions on participation, temporary suspension, or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Actions should be proportionate to the circumstances. Retaliation against people who report concerns in good faith is not acceptable.

Responsibilities of Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, responding to reports where practical, and applying the policy consistently. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so is appropriate and safe.
